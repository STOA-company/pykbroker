## Contracts

- `/oauth2/token` receives form-encoded appkey, appsecretkey, grant_type, and scope; token state can be reused.
- Reads and writes send `Input_0`, Bearer authorization, and x-client-id, never x-client-secret.
- Quote: `/krstock/quote/v1/currentPrice`; response `iem_cd` must match when supplied.
- Balance/holdings: `/krstock/inquiry/v1/balance`; `cts`/`cts_flag` and 00165/00218 follow all pages.
- Final balance page supplies totals; missing/repeated cursor or conflicting flag fails closed at 100 pages.
- Same-symbol holding rows combine quantity/value and weighted cost; ordinary cash sellable is fetched once.
- Sellable quantity: `/krstock/inquiry/v1/sellableQuantity` with `cfd_lon_cd=00`.
- Cash buyable: `/krstock/inquiry/v1/buyableQuantity`, `ost_dit_cd=1`, explicit ordinary limit price, `csh_orr_pbl_qty`.
- `/n2/acctinfo` verifies ownership: acct_type 01/02 use live, 03 uses mock for subsequent reads.
- Limit cash buy/sell: `/krstock/order/v1/cashBuy` and `/cashSell`; ordinary quote `01`, condition `00`, cash sell `00`, whole won. Venue validates product-specific ticks (the stock ladder would reject valid ETFs).
- Placement IDs are `mkt:<mkt_orr_no>` with numeric normalization; `/krstock/order/v1/cancel` accepts only these IDs, targets one market order number and `cor_qty` shares.
- Today's `/krstock/inquiry/v1/dailyOrderExecution` uses Seoul date and returns 1–11 digit `itg:<itg_orr_no>` IDs. The specification does not link market and integrated numbers; a placed order's status stays unknown until the caller has a matching integrated ID.
- Ledger state stays unknown for zero quantity, modify/cancel/rejection markers, cancelled or corrected quantities, and parent orders touched by original or SOR child rows. Child references are compared after numeric normalization (including zero or space padding).
- `cts_flag` is stripped and uppercased; blank means absent. Repeated or missing cursor and over 100 pages fail closed.
- Continuation codes and IGW40043 are stripped before comparison; padded codes cannot truncate balance pages.

## Error policy and evidence

- HTTP 200 alone does not establish success; required blocks and numeric fields must exist.
- `dca=0` with `orr_pbl_amt=0` is a valid zero balance only with a complete response.
- Successful read codes require the expected block; unknown codes are rejected even with completion wording. Known failures and malformed messages fail closed.
- HTTP 401 or IGW40043 clears only the rejected token; a newer shared token is reused, otherwise one refresh/replay needs a secret.
- Older valid local tokens cannot overwrite fresher shared tokens; malformed shared expiry is treated as absent.
- Redirects are refused before credentials can reach a second host.
- Writes classify 401 and IGW40043 as explicit authorization refusals. Timeout, 5xx, malformed acknowledgement and unverified business codes are unknown; no write is replayed after transmission.
- Replies without a known `rsp_cd` are rejected even with completion wording; writes also require `Output_0.mkt_orr_no`.
- HTTP 429 does not refresh or retry; read calls share a per-credential four-per-second limiter.
- Vendor bodies and credentials are absent from errors; tests use fake transport only.
- No live account or order call occurred; actual vendor rows and the market-to-integrated number relation remain unobserved.
